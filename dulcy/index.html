Optical Physics

Why does your lens's sweet spot matter?

Every photographic lens exhibits optical aberrations (spherical, chromatic, and coma) when wide open. Stopping down significantly reduces these flaws, but closing too far (like f/16 or f/22) introduces optical diffraction that irreversibly softens resolution and fine detail.

The sweet spot is the sweet aperture range—typically 2 to 3 stops down from maximum aperture—where glass delivers its most breathtaking edge-to-edge sharpness. Dulcy gives you this optimal number instantly, saving you from guessing or consulting complex MTF charts in the middle of a shoot.
