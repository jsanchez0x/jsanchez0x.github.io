Optical Physics

Why does your lens's sweet spot matter?

Every lens exhibits optical aberrations when shot wide open. Stopping down minimizes these flaws, but closing too far (like f/16 or f/22) introduces diffraction that softens fine details. The sweet spot is that golden aperture range where your glass delivers peak resolution from corner to corner.

f/1.4 - f/2.8 Optical Aberrations
f/5.6 - f/8 ★ Optimal Sweet Spot
f/16 - f/22 Diffraction Softening
